Compute — 4 topics

  1. EC2 — virtual machines

    A rented Linux box. You choose the instance family and size, manage the OS, and pay by the second. Still the right answer when you need full control or have something that does not containerise — and the baseline everything else is compared against.

  2. Containers — ECS, Fargate & EKS

    ECS is AWS’s own orchestrator; Fargate removes the servers underneath it; EKS is managed Kubernetes. For a small team Fargate is usually the sweet spot — you ship an image and never think about an instance again.

  3. Lambda & serverless

    Functions that run on demand and cost nothing when idle. Excellent for event-driven work — an S3 upload triggering a thumbnail job — and a poor fit for long-running or latency-critical paths because of cold starts and the execution time limit.

  4. Load balancing & auto scaling

    An Application Load Balancer spreads HTTP traffic across healthy targets and terminates TLS; an auto scaling group adds and removes instances against a metric. Together they are what "scale out" actually means in practice.
