  1. Regions, AZs & edge locations

    A region is a geographic area; an availability zone is an isolated datacentre within it; edge locations are the CDN’s points of presence. Spreading across AZs is how you survive a datacentre failure, and it is the cheapest availability you can buy.

  2. IAM — identity & access

    Users, groups, roles and policies — and the rule that matters: grant least privilege, and use roles rather than long-lived access keys. A leaked key is the single most common way AWS accounts get compromised and billed into five figures.

  3. The shared responsibility model

    AWS secures the cloud; you secure what you put in it. They handle the hypervisor, the physical datacentre and the managed service internals — you handle IAM, security groups, encryption choices, patching your own instances, and your application code.

    Four ways to talk to AWS. Click in the console to learn, then never do it again for anything that matters — infrastructure you cannot recreate from code is infrastructure you cannot recover. boto3 is the Python SDK; Terraform or CDK is how real environments are defined.
