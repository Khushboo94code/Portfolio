Infrastructure & Security — 5 topics

  1. Proxies — forward & reverse

    A forward proxy sits in front of clients, a reverse proxy in front of servers — same mechanism, opposite direction, completely different purpose. The reverse proxy is the one you deploy: TLS termination, static files, routing, buffering slow clients.

  2. Load balancers — L4 vs L7

    An L4 balancer forwards by IP and port without reading the payload; an L7 balancer parses HTTP and can route by path, header or cookie. L7 is more capable and more expensive per request — this is the networking view of the HLD load-balancing topic.

  3. CDN & the edge

    Caching content close to users so most requests never reach your origin. Push versus pull CDNs, cache keys and invalidation, and edge termination of TLS — which also cuts the handshake round trips users pay for.

  4. Firewalls, VPN & network isolation

    Controlling who can reach what: security groups and network ACLs in a cloud VPC, public versus private subnets, bastion hosts, and VPN tunnels. The principle is that a database should not have a public IP at all, rather than having one that's password-protected.

  5. Network attacks & defence

    What goes wrong deliberately: DDoS and volumetric floods, man-in-the-middle when TLS is absent or mis-validated, DNS spoofing, and SSRF where your own server is tricked into making a request. Rate limiting and strict egress rules are the everyday defences.
