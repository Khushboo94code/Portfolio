Application Layer & Web — 6 topics

The layer you work at every day. This group overlaps most with what you already build.

  1. HTTP fundamentals

    Methods and their semantics — safe, idempotent, cacheable — status codes that mean something specific, headers, and the request/response shape. Knowing which methods are idempotent is what makes retry logic correct rather than dangerous.

  2. HTTP/2 & HTTP/3

    HTTP/2 adds multiplexing and header compression over one TCP connection, which kills the old sharding hacks but not head-of-line blocking — TCP still stalls every stream on one lost packet. HTTP/3 moves to QUIC over UDP specifically to fix that.

  3. HTTPS, TLS & certificates

    The handshake, how certificate chains establish trust, and what TLS 1.3 removed to save a round trip. Practically: where TLS terminates in your architecture, what a reverse proxy does with it, and why certificate expiry is a recurring outage cause.

  4. Cookies, sessions & CORS

    How state survives a stateless protocol, and the browser rules that constrain it: the same-origin policy, cookie attributes (HttpOnly, Secure, SameSite), and CORS preflight. Most "CORS errors" are really a server not answering the preflight.

  5. WebSockets, SSE & polling

    Four ways to push data to a client, with real trade-offs: short polling is simple and wasteful, long polling holds a request open, SSE is one-directional over plain HTTP, and WebSockets give a full-duplex connection at the cost of stateful servers.
